About Eliya Production

Crafting Unrivaled Live Spectacles That Echo Across Pakistan.

At Eliya Production, we believe every stage is a canvas for emotion, laughter, and timeless cultural celebration. Based in Pakistan and renowned for staging hallmark events at the prestigious Arts Council of Pakistan, Karachi, our production team brings together creative vision, cutting-edge acoustic engineering, and high-impact visual design.

From our signature blend of soul-stirring Sufi Qawwali and family stand-up comedy in "Sham-e-Ishq o Mizah" to star-studded laughter galas and corporate award nights, we handle every detail—from artist booking and stage architecture to multi-camera 4K broadcast recording.

How We Execute

Our 4-Step Production Pipeline

From initial creative brainstorming to the final standing ovation.

01

Concept & Talent Curation

We define the thematic format, curate ideal artist pairings (Qawwals, comedians, vocalists), and finalize venue scheduling.

02

Stagecraft & Engineering

Architecting the physical stage, designing lighting plots, tuning acoustic arrays, and configuring multi-cam broadcast rigs.

03

Ticketing & Media Rollout

Digital promotion across Instagram, TikTok, and Facebook, alongside seamless online ticket booking via Ticketwala.

04

Live Show & 4K Master

Precision live execution, guest hospitality, stage management, followed by cinema-grade 4K post-production reels.
